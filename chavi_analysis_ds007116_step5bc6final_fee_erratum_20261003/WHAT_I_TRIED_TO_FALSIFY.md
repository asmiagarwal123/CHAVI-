# What I tried to falsify

1. **Claim: joint refitting improved S1 RMS coverage.** I checked the repaired B3-R confirmatory performance rows rather than an earlier narrative. P2 was 1,606/2,000 and P3 1,835/2,000 available-and-covered RMS intervals. The claim survives only for this S1 cell; the manuscript states that P3 remains below 0.95 and makes no universal claim.

2. **Claim: the empirical P3 RMS interval was 47.4% wider.** I recomputed widths from unrounded C2 bounds: P2 = 1.1978314626167037 - 0.8105956091766748 = 0.3872358534400289; P3 = 1.3717392219336024 - 0.8007996882411338 = 0.5709395336924686; ratio = 1.4743973953354241. The rounded claim survives. It is labelled descriptive and not isolated reference variance or superiority.

3. **Claim: coverage alone is inadequate for S5.** I checked repaired B3-R denominators: P3 had 1,260 available of 2,000 and 1,205 covered, so conditional coverage = 1,205/1,260 = 0.956349 and available-and-covered = 1,205/2,000 = 0.6025. The claim survives; availability is displayed beside coverage.

I also looked for unsupported causal, clinical, individual-reliability, task-equivalence, precise-elapsed-time, and universal-calibration statements. The manuscript restricts its language to an earlier/later empirical illustration and explicitly disclaims those interpretations.
