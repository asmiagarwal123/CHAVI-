# Figure captions

**Figure 1. Primary empirical intervals.** Point estimates and 95% percentile bootstrap intervals for the 52-pair M0/MAD analysis, shown on endpoint-specific axes. P2 holds the fitted transformation fixed; P3 resamples identities and refits it. Each interval used 999/999 valid draws. Differences are descriptive and do not isolate a pure reference-variance component.

**Figure 2. Confirmatory coverage–availability tradeoff.** Conditional coverage (circles, with ±1.96 stored Monte Carlo SE) and interval availability (squares) for MAD-scale RMS displacement percentile intervals under confirmatory S1 and S5. The dashed line is nominal 0.95 coverage. Conditional coverage is among available intervals; availability is among 2,000 attempted outer replicates.

**Figure 3. Sensitivity point estimates.** Prespecified M0/SD, matched-support M0/M1, fixed strict-V, and reselected strict-V point estimates with denominators. Panels use endpoint-specific axes; no inferential bars are available or invented. Cross-analysis differences combine distinct changes in model, scale, support, eligibility, or pair selection.
