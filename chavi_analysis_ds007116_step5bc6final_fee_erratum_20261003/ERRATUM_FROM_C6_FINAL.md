# Publication-information erratum from C6-FINAL

## Corrected statement

**Old statement:** “The published individual fee for a PLOS ONE ‘all other articles’ submission is **US$2,382**.”

**Replacement:** “**Fee to be confirmed with PLOS before submission.** On 2026-10-03, the live official PLOS fees page displayed ‘All other articles’ at **US$2,477**. The page also states that fees vary by journal and the rate effective on the submission date applies. PLOS ONE's submission guidelines link to that page, but the accessible official extraction did not retain the PLOS ONE heading next to the fee row; the $2,477 row is therefore documented but not asserted as this manuscript's applicable fee.”

Source accessed 2026-10-03: <https://plos.org/fees/>. PLOS ONE links to this fee page in its official submission guidance: <https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/submission-guidelines>.
