# Publication route decision

## Verdict B

**Stronger as an applied/reproducibility paper with simulation support.**

The project is publishable after routine author and journal review if it retains this route. Its evidence is strongest for an auditable, failure-aware evaluation of two implemented resampling workflows and a worked Penn LEAD sensitivity illustration. It is not sufficient to introduce a generally calibrated bootstrap method or to attribute P2/P3 differences solely to estimated-reference uncertainty. P1’s subnominal behavior and endpoint/mechanism dependence require that restraint.

The remaining measurement metadata are limitations rather than blockers because the revised paper uses recorded-field and occasion language and makes no clinical, developmental, equivalence, or normative claim.

**One next action:** have the author team choose a journal whose applied-methods/reproducibility scope fits this contribution, then format the revised draft to its reporting and word-limit requirements without broadening claims.
