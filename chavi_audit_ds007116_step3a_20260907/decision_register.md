- **Primary priority — settled:** longitudinal reliability/dependability and profile level versus profile shape.
- **Supporting priority — settled:** change and occasion/developmental effects for interpretation.
- **Prediction — conditional exploratory:** only beyond baseline outcome, relevant covariates, and global cognition after specification freeze.
- **Task-level route — recommended:** the five released proxy fields are auditable narrow measurements; broad domains remain conditional.
- **QC — recommended pending approval:** documented valid/likely-valid with all applicable QC; strict V-only and unresolved-inclusive sensitivities.
- **Pairing — provisional:** first two eligible primary candidate; scheduled ses-1/ses-2 sensitivity; all unordered pairs reported.
- **Digit Symbol — settled mapping correction:** current candidate-domain configuration places it under Executive control/processing speed; prior Complex-cognition assignment is historical only.
- **Scoped blockers:** LNB denominator/QC scope, ADT unit conflict, task-version comparability, PCET/AIM/Trails domain claims, direction/weighting/age policy.
- **Not run:** composite scoring, standardization, age fitting, reliability, SEM/MDC, prediction, clinical claims, or Evidence Engine.
