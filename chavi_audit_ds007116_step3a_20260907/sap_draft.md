# CHAVI SAP draft — reliability-first

## Primary
Longitudinal dependability/agreement of prespecified cognitive measurements and individualized profile shape, distinguishing overall profile level from relative within-person patterns.

## Supporting
Systematic change and occasion/developmental effects relevant to interpreting longitudinal dependability. Change, practice, occasion, and measurement error remain distinct.

## Conditional exploratory
Incremental predictive validity beyond baseline outcome, relevant covariates, and global cognition, only after the measurement specification and cohort rules are frozen.

## Specification and cohorts
Use the executable JSON specification. Fixed fields must remain identical across occasions. The first-two-eligible cognitive-pair cohort is separate from the ses-1-based profile/outcome cohort. Report all unordered, first-two, and complete scheduled pairs separately; overlapping pairs are not independent participants.

## Missingness, QC, comparability
Report score missingness, QC exclusion, and comparability uncertainty separately. Explicit invalidity overrides unresolved/missing QC in inclusive sensitivities. The continuation session is not a new CNB administration.

## Future methods after approval
Freeze direction, weighting, transformation, age handling, follow-up rule, and agreement model before any composite, reliability, SEM/MDC, or prediction work. Standardization/age adjustment may be considered only with a justified reference and leakage-safe fitting.

## Block scope
LNB denominator/QC scope blocks its transformed or broad interpretation, not raw-score auditing; ADT units block its disputed interpretation; PCET/AIM/Trails placement blocks their domain claims; version/comparability uncertainty blocks equivalence claims; these do not automatically block unrelated task-level analyses or the entire study.
