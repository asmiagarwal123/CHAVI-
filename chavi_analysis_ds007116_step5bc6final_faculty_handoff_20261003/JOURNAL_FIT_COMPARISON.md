# Journal-fit comparison (verified 2026-10-03)

## 1. PLOS ONE — provisional recommendation

**Fit and article route.** PLOS ONE assesses original research for technical soundness, supported conclusions, ethics, and data-availability standards. Its broad scope and research-article route are compatible with a cautious methods/simulation paper with an empirical illustration; this is a fit assessment, not an eligibility determination.

**Access, code, and data.** PLOS requires data and related metadata underlying findings in an appropriate public repository unless already provided at submission, and its code policy expects author-generated analytical code supporting results to be freely available. The present package does not meet that release requirement until the author team approves a lawful archival plan.

**Fees and support.** The published individual fee for a PLOS ONE “all other articles” submission is **US$2,382**, subject to the fee applicable on submission. PLOS describes institutional partnership models, Research4Life support, and case-by-case Publication Fee Assistance requested at submission. No author eligibility, waiver, or institutional agreement is asserted here.

Official sources, accessed 2026-10-03: [journal information](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/journal-information), [submission guidelines](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/submission-guidelines), [data policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/data-availability), [code policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/code-availability), and [fees/funding](https://plos.org/publish/fees/).

## 2. BMC Medical Research Methodology — specialist alternative

**Fit and article route.** BMC Medical Research Methodology publishes original research on methodological approaches to healthcare research, including statistical methods and studies of how methodological choices affect results. Its scope is closer to the methodological core than BMJ Open. Faculty should judge whether the clinical/cognitive illustration establishes sufficient healthcare relevance.

**Access, code, and data.** The journal's current author guidance and declarations/data-availability requirements must be completed against the final chosen article type. This package supplies permitted aggregate outputs only; it makes no claim that protected source data or analytical code are publicly archived.

**Fees and support.** Springer Nature directs authors to the journal's Open Access Fees and Funding section/current APC list, which can change. A current numeric APC was not reliably displayed in the official material accessed for this review, so it is deliberately recorded as **not verified** rather than guessed. Any funder or institutional route must be checked by the authors.

Official sources, accessed 2026-10-03: [aims and scope](https://bmcmedresmethodol.biomedcentral.com/submission-guidelines/aims-and-scope), [submission guidelines](https://bmcmedresmethodol.biomedcentral.com/submission-guidelines), and [Springer Nature APC guidance](https://support.springernature.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000227571-find-the-article-processing-charges-apc-for-a-specific-journal).

## 3. BMJ Open — broad applied alternative

**Fit and article route.** BMJ Open considers secondary analyses, modelling studies, pilot studies, and methods work relevant to patients, clinicians, or healthcare practice. Its clinical/practice relevance requirement makes the fit less direct for this methods-first paper.

**Access, code, and data.** Research articles require a data-availability statement; BMJ encourages appropriate datasets to be made available where legally and ethically possible. Authors must write an accurate statement for the final release conditions.

**Fees and support.** BMJ Open lists an APC of **GBP 2,535** (exclusive of applicable VAT/GST) for all article types, no submission/colour/page charge, possible institutional agreements, and waiver/discount processes subject to its stated conditions. No eligibility or agreement is asserted here.

Official sources, accessed 2026-10-03: [aims and scope](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/pages/research-aims-and-scope) and [authors, fees, waivers, data sharing, and article guidance](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/pages/authors).

## Provisional decision

Develop the PLOS ONE route first only after faculty endorse the applied/reproducibility framing and the author team approves an authorized code/permitted-aggregate-data archival plan. The author team, not this package, chooses the journal and fee route.
