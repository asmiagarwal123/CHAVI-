## Unresolved Claims
- Task-version equivalence across time points.
- Adequacy of a linear age model.
- Final profile reliability estimands.
- Centered-profile mathematical stability.

## Prohibited Claims
These claims are explicitly prohibited within the context of CHAVI:
- Clinical validity.
- Diagnostic value (e.g. ADHD/ASD classifier).
- Validated broad cognitive domains.
- Population norms.
- Confirmed practice effects.
- Confirmed developmental effects.
- Individual clinical strengths or weaknesses.
- Validated Evidence Engine thresholds.
- Prediction performance.
- External generalization outside the current dataset.
