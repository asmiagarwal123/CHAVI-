# Agreement versus consistency review

ICC(A,1) is sensitive to systematic occasion shifts because it asks whether scores agree in absolute level. ICC(C,1) discounts a common shift and therefore addresses consistency of ordering rather than absolute equality. Correlations measure association only and do not by themselves establish agreement.
