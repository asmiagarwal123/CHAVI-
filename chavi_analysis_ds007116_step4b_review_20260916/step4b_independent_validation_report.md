## Scientific interpretation

Absolute agreement, consistency, correlation, and change are distinct quantities. Differences between ICC(A,1) and ICC(C,1) are consistent with occasion shifts for some tasks. Positive mean changes are descriptive later-minus-earlier differences only.

## Unsupported explanations

The results do not identify practice, development, intervention, clinical change, broad-domain validity, or profile validity. The five fields are task-level proxy measurements, not validated cognitive domains.
