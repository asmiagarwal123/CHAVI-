# Multiplicity and SEM/MDC Recommendations

## Multiplicity Handling
Because the sample size is small ($N=52$) and profile components are inherently correlated, standard Bonferroni correction is overly punitive, yet doing nothing inflates false claims.
**Recommendation**: Prioritize the reporting of fully bootstrapped 95% Confidence Intervals for Level, Scatter, and RMSE. Binary significance testing (p-values) should be strictly demoted to secondary or omitted to prevent inflated claims.

## SEM and MDC Interpretation
Calculating the Standard Error of Measurement (SEM) and Minimum Detectable Change (MDC) for individual profile shape is challenging due to real developmental change over varying longitudinal intervals in the LEAD dataset.
**Recommendation**: Treat SEM/MDC as conditional/descriptive. If reported, they must carry a strong caveat that they assume a static true score, which is likely false for adolescents over long developmental gaps.
